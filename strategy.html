THE FLAGSHIP METHOD

Fibonacci is not an accessory.
It is the map.

The MA Strategy is a rules-based framework built around higher-timeframe context, meaningful market structure, liquidity and a defined Fibonacci impulse. Fibonacci gives the model its location; structure and liquidity determine whether that location deserves attention.

CORE CONCEPT · 02

Why each entry exists

The model has more than one way to enter because confirmation and price location answer different questions.

01

0.71 Direct Entry

Purpose: capture the planned retracement at the predefined Fibonacci POI when the full setup is already qualified. This is the cleanest expression of the model: context first, impulse second, Fibonacci third.

  • HTF bias is aligned.
  • Meaningful swing is identified.
  • Fib is drawn 1 → 0.
  • Price reaches 0.71.
  • Risk is predefined before execution.
02

Liquidity-Sweep Entry

Purpose: improve confirmation when price reaches the 0.71 area but the trader wants evidence that liquidity has been taken and rejected before entering.

  • Price reaches the POI.
  • Liquidity is visible nearby.
  • Price sweeps / raids it.
  • Reaction and structure support the idea.
  • Entry follows confirmation, not the wick alone.
03

Structure-Shift Confirmation

Purpose: answer whether the reaction has actually changed local order flow. A wick is an event; a confirmed shift is evidence of a change in short-term structure.

  • Wait for a meaningful closed candle.
  • Identify the relevant internal swing.
  • Require displacement where appropriate.
  • Use the shift to refine, not to invent a setup.
