02 · DEEP DIVE

AMD — Accumulation · Manipulation · Distribution

AMD is taught as a market-behaviour framework, not a magic three-box pattern. We study where liquidity builds, how price can raid it, and what evidence supports continuation or reversal.

ACCUMULATIONRange / liquidity builds
MANIPULATIONLiquidity raid / displacement
DISTRIBUTIONExpansion / delivery
↘
01Accumulation

Price compresses or rotates while liquidity becomes increasingly obvious around the range.

02Manipulation

A sweep or raid can remove liquidity. The sweep alone is not an entry; context and reaction matter.

03Distribution

Once structure and displacement support the idea, price can deliver toward the next meaningful liquidity area.
